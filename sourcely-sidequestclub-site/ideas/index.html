10 ideas to kick off your tiny business today

These are starting points, not promises. Each one has a first step small enough to finish today, and a rough idea of what it costs to begin.

1

Print-on-demand shirts or mugs

A print service makes and ships each item after someone orders it, so you hold no stock.

Today: sketch three simple designs for one niche you know well. Cost to start: low.

2

Thrifted or vintage resale

Buy secondhand clothes or home goods, clean them up and resell online.

Today: list five things you already own and photograph them in daylight. Cost to start: low.

3

Small-batch candles, soap or snacks

Make a small batch at home and sell it to people you know first.

Today: make one test batch and write down the cost of every ingredient. Cost to start: low. Check local rules for anything you eat or put on skin.

4

Freelance a skill you already have

Editing, design, video cuts, spreadsheets, translation: someone needs it done.

Today: write one sentence that says who you help and with what, then post it. Cost to start: close to nothing.

5

Digital templates

Planners, budget sheets, resume or social media templates that sell again and again.

Today: turn a file you made for yourself into a clean template. Cost to start: close to nothing.

6

Tutoring or coaching

Teach what you are good at, one hour at a time.

Today: pick one subject and tell three people you are taking students. Cost to start: close to nothing.

7

Custom stickers and stationery

Small, light, easy to ship and easy to give as a gift.

Today: draw one design and order a small test run. Cost to start: low.

8

A local service

Dog walking, plant care, organizing, tutoring neighbors' kids. People pay for time they do not have.

Today: message ten neighbors with one clear offer and a price. Cost to start: close to nothing.

9

Curated gift boxes

Pick five good things for a specific person, such as a new dog owner or a first-year student, and package them.

Today: write the person you are making it for, then list five items. Cost to start: low to medium.

10

Private-label one simple product

Order a plain product from a manufacturer and add your own packaging or branding. Start with a tiny order.

Today: choose the product, then look at where it is made. Cost to start: medium. Order about 20, not 500.

pick one. do the "Today" step. that's it.

Not sure which to pick? Choose the idea where you already know the customer. Then test it for under $50.
