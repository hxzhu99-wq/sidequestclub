The factory map

If you are looking for starting your own brands, here is what you need to know about where to find high quality "made in China" factories.

China's factories are not spread evenly. Each region has clusters of makers in one kind of product. If you know where your product is made, your search gets much shorter.

Simplified, hand-drawn map for orientation only. Marker positions are approximate, and each cluster covers a wider area than its dot. This shows where industries gather, not which factories are good. Verify any supplier yourself before paying.

How to use this

1. Pick your product type. Use the filters to see the clusters near your product.

2. Search with a place name. Add the city to your search, for example “silicone kitchenware Foshan”.

3. Ask who makes it. Many sellers in big markets are traders, not makers. Ask which factory produces your item. See how to test an idea first.
