3 ways to test an idea for under $50

An idea is a guess. A test turns it into a number. All three tests below cost less than $50, and none needs a finished product.

rule zero: write your pass mark first.

Before you start, decide what result means yes. For example, “5 people agree to pay”. If you decide afterward, you will always find a reason to keep going.

1. Pre-sell it

Describe the product with a photo or a mock-up, name a price, and ask people to buy before it exists. Post it where your customers already are: a group chat, a community, your own account.

Needs: one picture, one sentence, one price. Pass mark example: 5 people ask how to pay.

2. Make a tiny batch

Make or order 3 to 5 units and sell them to people who are not your close friends. Friends say yes to be kind. Strangers tell you the truth.

Needs: materials for a handful of units. Pass mark example: all of them sell, and at least one buyer comes back with a question or a reorder.

3. Run a fake-door page

Build a one-page site that describes the product and has a “Join the waitlist” button. Share it and count how many visitors leave an email. A small paid ad can give you a first batch of visitors.

Needs: a simple page builder and a small ad budget. Pass mark example: 20 sign-ups from the visitors you paid for.
